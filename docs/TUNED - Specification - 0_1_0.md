# TUNED Specification

Version 0.1.0 working draft | 2026-08-30
Framework: TUNED (Tuned to the Utterance, Not the Established Default)
Inherits from: CRAFT (the six conditions and enforcement layer), ORE (ingestion boundary), STRUCK (exit boundary), Frame Language (vocabulary discipline), Precision-First Design Standard (operational definability).
Status: working draft. This specification governs the process of running an independent writing agent to declared parameters, not the evaluation of the prose as such. Condition 1 is specified in full; Conditions 2 through 6 and Sections 5 through 8 are filled to first-draft depth against that process and are open for the author's pass.

---

## 1. Scope

TUNED is a writing-quality instrument for public writing produced with an AI. The whole is generative: it produces prose through an independent writing agent. This specification does not evaluate that prose as such. It governs the process of running that agent to a set of declared writing parameters: that the parameters going in are legible and complete, that the agent runs independent of the larger context, that its verification is not self-adjudicated, and that a legible record of the parameters being met comes back out. That process is the CRAFT domain application. The prose, and the parameters it is written to, are TUNED's own.

The division of ownership. TUNED owns the writing. It supplies the writing parameters (the median-pull compensation, the bands, the register, the target) going in, and verifies on the way out that they were met. TUNED also owns the tuning of the CRAFT, ORE, and STRUCK configurations themselves; it is the tuner of the harness, not only the supplier of parameters to it. CRAFT, ORE, and STRUCK own the process integrity of running an independent agent to those parameters, and nothing about the prose beyond that.

The configuration constraint. TUNED's tuning of the harness configurations operates independently of the model's live pulls. The configurations are grounded in the researched base-pull factors, the median-pull inventory and the behavioral profile, which are external and pre-established; configuration is data-driven from that research, never judgment-driven from the model at runtime. The guard against the pulls is not set by the pulls. This is why the profile must be measured behaviorally, not self-reported, and measured on real public writing, since it is the harness's configuration source and not merely a reference.

Scope limit: public writing only. Prose destined for a reader whose judgment carries weight is in scope; the private-to-public crossing is the trigger. Conversation, code, and private working documents are out.

TUNED shifts the saying, not the thinking. It governs how a thing is said and never what the author has come to understand about it. Conceptual work, the substance of an argument, and the maturation of an idea are the author's and are out of scope.

This specification operates at the domain-application level of CRAFT. It inherits all of CRAFT's normative requirements except where Section 3 declares an adaptation under CRAFT Section 8, and it adds the process-domain requirements derived from the inside analysis in Section 2.

## 2. Inside analysis: the domain's failure modes

CRAFT Section 8 requires a domain application to derive its requirements from inside analysis of the domain's evaluation failure modes rather than from mechanical translation. The analysis is recorded in the companion documents and summarized here.

The root failure is over-functioning: producing more than the situation warrants, in any direction. The specific tells are species of it. The full set of ten, each with a detect signature and a counter, is the median-pull inventory (companion document). Over-claiming is over-functioning epistemically; sterility is over-functioning the correction; the overblown draft is over-functioning the life-restoration. Because the failure runs in both directions on every axis, each counter is a band, a floor and a ceiling around a calibrated midpoint, and the failure is leaving the band either way.

Over-functioning is largely pressure-driven: pressure from the larger context leaks into the writing. This is why the process, not the prose, is what the harness bounds. An independent agent, isolated from the session's accumulated pressure and drift, over-functions far less at the source, so isolation is the first line and compensation is the smaller second line on the residue. Two structural facts follow and shape the conditions below. First, the wrapper runs on the same model whose pulls it removes, so the model's bias operates inside its own correction; the correction cannot be self-adjudicated. Second, the brief itself is a pressure source, so it must stay calm and minimal.

The full analysis and its validation on a real article are in the companion capture document.

## 3. Inheritance receipt

Per CRAFT Section 10, the inheritance under which this domain application is built:

- CRAFT, the six conditions and the enforcement layer (adversarial gate, non-self-adjudication, feedback), as specialized in Section 4. CRAFT bounds the process: the writing runs independent of the larger context, and its verification is not self-adjudicated.
- ORE at the ingestion boundary (Section 5): the parameters going in are legible and complete, a thin target graded and recorded as thin, never silently defaulted.
- STRUCK at the exit boundary (Section 5): the output returns a legible record that the parameters were met, checkable by the author, plus the over-claim guard on any claim-bearing parts.
- Frame Language, governing the vocabulary of this specification and of the tuned output.
- Precision-First Design Standard, holding every criterion to operational definability.

Section 8 adaptations, declared per CRAFT Section 8, which permits omission or adaptation with a justified equivalence argument:

- Non-self-adjudication (CRAFT Section 7.2) is realized by the author's recognition-and-correction loop and, where available, a separate evaluative lens, rather than by ex-ante third-party attestation. Justification: the author is the party whose voice is the target and is therefore the authority, disinterested toward the model, on whether the output is theirs; the model, being the producer, cannot hold that authority over its own output. This is a realization of the invariant, not a weakening: the overturning basis is not controlled by the producer.
- The full evaluation-chain apparatus (population-level calibration, deployment-scale declarations) is scoped out where the domain is single-author, single-piece, and declared as such. Justification: over-importing the apparatus would over-function the standard onto a writing tool, the domain's own root failure.

## 4. The six conditions, specified for the process

### Condition 1 (Decision Context)

The decision the process supports must be specified before the writing agent is dispatched: whether to accept the agent's output as meeting the declared writing parameters, or to re-dispatch, given that the output was produced by a process run independent of the larger context. The following elements are specified at this stage and carried into every downstream condition.

**The parties.** The author directs the process and owns the output. The independent writing agent is the instrument. The reader is the audience whose judgment the writing must meet.

**The independence requirement.** The writing agent runs isolated from the larger context: a fresh agent carrying only the parameters and the piece, not the session's accumulated pressure or drift. Independence is the point of the harness, because over-functioning is context-pressure leaking into the writing. An output produced without that isolation does not satisfy this condition, however good the prose reads.

**Validity conditions, stated so reality can refute them.** The process is a good input to the decision when the parameters were legible and complete going in (recorded by the ingestion grading, Section 5), when the agent ran isolated, when the verification was not self-adjudicated, and when a legible record of the parameters being met came back out. It is invalid when the target was too thin to aim at, when the piece is private, when what the piece needs is conceptual work rather than register work, or when the output was produced inside the larger context rather than by an isolated agent.

**Out-of-scope declaration.** Private writing. The thinking: the substance, the claims' correctness, and the maturation of an idea. The prose as such: this specification evaluates the process of producing it against the parameters, not the prose on its own literary merits.

**Risk-bearers.** The author bears the loss when the output reads as not-them, as machine-written, or as over-claiming: their voice, their presence, and their standing with the reader are exposed. The reader bears the loss when the output over-claims or misleads. Both are named here and carried; a check that does not account for the author's presence or the reader's exposure is out of compliance with this decision context.

**Risk reduction.** TUNED reduces the risk that public writing produced with an AI reads as averaged rather than authored, and that it over-functions past what its author would stand behind. It reduces this for the author, whose particularity the median erases, and for the reader, who is owed writing that does not over-claim.

**The parameters as carried input.** The target register, the concern-model, the intent, and the context are declared here and graded for uncertainty per Section 5. A thin target is recorded as a named uncertainty and carried into the output, never silently defaulted to a safe middle.

**Adversarial threat model.** The primary adversary is the instrument itself: the model's pulls operate inside the correction, so the threat is the bias defeating its own removal, in either direction (sterile or overblown). Pressure is a named threat class: a heavy brief or an accumulated session induces the over-functioning the process means to prevent, which is why isolation is required. A thin or absent target is a threat class: it invites the silent default the ingestion boundary exists to catch. Configuration by the model's live pulls is a threat class: the guard set by the pulls is no guard, which is why the configuration is grounded in the pre-established research.

Condition 1 is prior to all others. Parameters aimed at before the decision context is specified are arbitrary; the register, the bands, the checks, and the independence requirement all derive from what is declared here.

### Condition 2 (Ontology)

The constructs the process measures, defined so two readers agree they are present. Two families. First, the writing parameters: the median-pull inventory, each pull operationally defined, with over-functioning as the organizing relation and the bands as the measured property; the register, context, and intent as declared, decomposable dimensions. Second, the process-integrity constructs: independence (the agent ran isolated from the larger context), non-self-adjudication (the overturning basis is not the producer), and configuration-grounding (the config traces to the researched base-pull factors, not the live pulls). Each is defined so its presence or absence is checkable from the run's record.

### Condition 3 (Instruments)

The detectors, with declared uncertainty. For the parameters: mechanical counts (em dashes, phrase density, opener concentration) carry low uncertainty; judgment lenses (is this sterile, is this over-claimed) carry high uncertainty and route to the author, per the non-self-adjudication adaptation. For the process: the isolation check (was a fresh agent dispatched with only the parameters and the piece) and the configuration-provenance check (does the config trace to the inventory and profile rather than to a runtime judgment) are structural and carry low uncertainty. An instrument that reads its own configuration off the model's live pulls is disqualified regardless of its measured accuracy.

### Condition 4 (Pre-Specified Criteria)

The criteria declared before the agent is dispatched. For the parameters: the bands, a floor and a ceiling per pull; the target register and its deltas. The over-functioning guard is the requirement that both bounds hold. For the process: the agent runs isolated; the configuration is drawn from the researched base-pull factors; the verification is not self-adjudicated. All are fixed before dispatch, so the run is judged against a standing bar rather than one set after seeing the output.

### Condition 5 (Decision Logic)

The accept-or-re-dispatch logic, written before outputs are produced. Parameters met and the process valid: accept. Parameters not met: re-dispatch on the parts in question, not the whole. Process invalid (target too thin, agent not isolated, verification self-adjudicated, config not grounded): the run does not count, independent of how the prose reads, and the fault is fixed before re-dispatch. The consequence level is the public-versus-private stakes and the audience, calibrating how hard the checks bite.

### Condition 6 (Feedback with Propagation)

The recognition-and-correction loop is the feedback mechanism: the author's edits are the signal, and a tuned agent is re-dispatched on the parts in question as propagation. Calibration drift is the pulls shifting by model era; it propagates not by the model re-reporting itself but by re-running the behavioral research, updating the inventory and the model profile, which are the configuration source. Feedback thus closes back onto the researched base-pull factors, never onto the live pulls.

## 5. Boundaries

ORE at the input: grade the uncertainty of the target, concern, intent, and context; no silent default on a thin input; expose the thinness so it is carried, not hidden. The parameters going into the agent must be legible and complete, or their incompleteness recorded.

STRUCK at the output: the run returns a legible record that the parameters were met, checkable by the author rather than asserted. On the claim-bearing parts only: no over-claim, state what would overturn a factual claim, trace it to origin. Voice and tone are out of STRUCK's scope; they are TUNED's own.

## 6. Construction grammar

Structural operativity: every check runs on every piece, cannot be silently bypassed, and is logged as part of the run's record. Detection paired with response for each pull and for each process-integrity construct. Direction validation: the author's directional origin (the recognition and correction) and the model's (the production) are both present and distinguished, so the output is never mistaken for the author's own composition.

## 7. Conformance

A conforming TUNED run produces a record from which the following are assessable: the parameters declared at Condition 1 and graded at Section 5; the isolation of the writing agent; the configuration's provenance in the researched base-pull factors; the per-pull band checks with their results; the STRUCK obligations on any claims; and the accept-or-re-dispatch decision with its basis. Non-conformance is any of these being absent or unassessable from the record.

## 8. The skill

TUNED ships as a writing-only skill. Its argument format is the Condition 1 inputs (the target, concern, and intent as declared, ORE-graded), and its output is a machine-readable record carrying the checks and the STRUCK obligations on claims. The dispatch discipline: a fresh isolated agent per run, carrying only the parameters and the piece; a calm, minimal brief, because over-briefing is itself pressure; and re-dispatch on the parts in question rather than the whole. The skill's configuration is loaded from the median-pull inventory and the model profile, never inferred at runtime.
