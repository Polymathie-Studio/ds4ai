# TUNED Calibration Corpus - Raw Texts Index

Version 0.1.0 | 2026-08-31

The raw prose gathered to calibrate the TUNED factors and tone primitives. Preserved here from the session scratchpad so it is a durable reference corpus rather than ephemeral temp. 148 files, roughly 34 authors, 282,931 words. Files are named `batch-<author>-N.txt` (and two `baseline-*` seeds). Two stems are second pieces by an author already present: `tressie-dolly` is a Tressie McMillan Cottom piece, `vitalik-legitimacy` a Vitalik Buterin piece.

Each author has a handful of pieces chosen to span the registers they write in, which is what lets the corpus show within-author mode-switching. This corpus doubles as the copybook library and the podcast guest map.

A technical band was later added to anchor the abstract, high-formality pole the substacks under-sampled: 15 curated academic pieces named `sci-<slug>.txt` (journal, article, and working-paper texts extracted from a mixed PDF collection, references stripped, books truncated to a 5000-word sample). They are from other writers, not the author's own corpus. With them the corpus is 163 pieces. The per-author table below covers the substack band only.

## Caveats

The spread is strong writers across many registers but few genuinely singular stylists; the modes and tone primitives are calibrated against this and want more singular voices to sharpen the extremes. Author identities beyond the stem are recorded in the calibration findings doc, not here, to keep this a plain file manifest.
