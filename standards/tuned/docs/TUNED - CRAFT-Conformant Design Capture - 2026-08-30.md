# TUNED - CRAFT-Conformant Design Capture

Version 0.1.0 | 2026-08-30

This captures where TUNED's design landed across the 2026-08-29 to 08-30 sessions, once it was bounded by the standards corpus. It supersedes the framing in the earlier structural spine on the points where they differ, and it points to the existing artifacts for detail rather than restating them.

## The structure: generative whole, evaluative middle

TUNED as a whole generates public writing. Its middle layer, the compensation and the checks and the recognition loop, evaluates a draft against pre-specified criteria. CRAFT evaluates and does not generate, so TUNED as a whole is not a CRAFT chain, but its evaluative middle is, wrapped around the generator. Making the bounds CRAFT-conformant means making that middle layer a CRAFT domain application. The generation is what the conformant bounds enclose.

Scope: public writing only. The private-to-public crossing is the trigger where getting the tone right starts to matter; conversation, code, and private working documents are out. TUNED shifts the saying, not the thinking: it tunes how a thing is said, never what the author has come to understand about it. That limit is stated plainly and belongs verbatim in the README.

## The root failure: over-functioning

The recurring failure under every specific tell is over-functioning: exceeding what the situation warrants, in any direction. The ten median-pulls are species of it. Over-claiming is over-functioning epistemically; sterility is over-functioning the de-slop; the overblown-punchy draft was over-functioning the life-restoration. So each counter is a band, a floor and a ceiling around a calibrated midpoint, and over-functioning is leaving the band either way. Over-functioning is the diagnosis that organizes the pulls; it does not replace them, or it floats.

Over-functioning is largely pressure-driven, which the author's own working-rhythm rule already names: pressure biases toward producing. A fresh, isolated agent carries none of the session's accumulated pressure and over-functions far less at the source, so isolation is the first line and compensation is the smaller second line on the residue. The dispatch itself must stay calm: an over-heavy brief is pressure and induces the over-functioning it meant to prevent.

## The bias problem and its fixes

The wrapper runs on the same model whose bias it exists to remove, so the bias operates inside the correction. The concrete run proved it: the de-slop over-corrected into sterile, then over-corrected into overblown. The fixes:

- Mechanical checks are the bias-proof part, because they do not run on the model's judgment. Push as much of the wrapper into mechanics as possible.
- Life-floors, not only slop-ceilings. Ceilings alone let over-correction sterilize past a floor. Symmetric bands bound both directions.
- A separate lens. A fresh adversarial pass catches the first pass's overreach because it is not attached to the cuts it made.
- The human recognition loop is the ultimate backstop, because a model cannot fully police its own bias. This is CRAFT's non-self-adjudication: the basis for overturning an output must not be controlled by its producer.

## The CRAFT-conformance mapping (the bounds as the six conditions)

The evaluative middle maps onto CRAFT's six conditions once tuning is cast as an evaluation of writing against pre-specified criteria:

- Condition 1, decision context: the target register and the concern-model; the risk-bearers named (the author, whose voice and reputation ride on the output, and the reader); and the saying-not-thinking line as an explicit out-of-scope declaration.
- Condition 2, ontology: the median-pull inventory, each pull operationally defined so two readers agree it is present.
- Condition 3, instruments: the detectors, with uncertainty declared. Mechanical counts carry low uncertainty; judgment lenses carry high uncertainty and therefore require the human.
- Condition 4, pre-specified criteria: the bands, floor and ceiling per pull, declared before the draft. This is the over-functioning guard.
- Condition 5, decision logic: the accept-or-revise logic and the consequence level, which is the public-versus-private stakes.
- Condition 6, feedback: the recognition-and-correction loop, with re-dispatch as propagation and calibration drift as the pulls shifting by model era.

At the boundaries: ORE at the input, grading the uncertainty of the target, intent, and context, never defaulting a thin target to a safe middle, exposing the thinness rather than pretending to know it. STRUCK on the claim-bearing output only, where the writing asserts facts: do not over-claim, state what would overturn the claim, trace it to origin. STRUCK does not bound voice or tone; that stays TUNED's own.

What conformance buys over mere resemblance: it forces the parts we kept rediscovering by hand. Non-self-adjudication makes the human loop the structural overturning basis. The adversarial gate makes a wrapper that never flags non-conformant. ORE forces grading a thin target. STRUCK forces the over-claim guard.

The caveat, honestly held: CRAFT is heavy, and full conformance would over-build a writing tool, the very failure being guarded against. CRAFT Section 8 is the release valve: a domain application may omit or adapt conditions with a justified equivalence argument. TUNED adopts the six as its bounding skeleton with declared writing-domain adaptations, the recognition loop as its non-self-adjudication realization being the clearest, rather than importing the entire evaluation apparatus.

## The harness checks the process, not the prose (settled 2026-08-30)

A reframe that relocates CRAFT, ORE, and STRUCK. They are process and legibility standards, not prose standards, so they apply to the process of running an independent writing agent, never to evaluating the prose as such. Pointed at the process, they fit, which is what dissolved the doubt about whether they fit at all.

The division of ownership:

- TUNED owns the writing. It supplies the writing parameters (the pulls, bands, register, target) going in, and verifies on the way out that those parameters were met. TUNED also owns the tuning of the CRAFT, ORE, and STRUCK configurations themselves; it is the tuner of the harness, not only the supplier of parameters to it.
- CRAFT, ORE, and STRUCK own the process integrity of running an independent agent to those parameters. ORE: the parameters going in are legible and complete, a thin one recorded as thin, never silently defaulted. CRAFT: the writing process runs independent of the larger context, isolated from its pressure and drift, its verification not self-adjudicated. STRUCK: the output returns a legible record that the parameters were met, checkable by the author rather than asserted.

The purpose of the harness is independence from the larger context. Over-functioning is pressure from that context leaking into the writing; an isolated, independent process is what keeps it out. CRAFT's chain-independence and non-self-adjudication are the standard form of the isolation-plus-human-check reached by trial in the concrete run.

The configuration constraint, which is the deepest form of the independence requirement: TUNED's tuning of the harness configurations must operate independently of the model's live pulls. The configurations are grounded in the researched base-pull factors, the median-pull inventory and the behavioral profile, which are external and pre-established. Configuration is data-driven from the research, never judgment-driven from the model at runtime. The guard against the pulls is not set by the pulls. This is why the profile must be measured behaviorally rather than self-reported (a self-reported config would be the pull configuring its own guard), and why it must be measured on real public writing, since the profile is the harness's configuration source and not merely a reference.

Consequence for the specification: the six conditions target the process, not the prose. Condition 1 is the writing job plus the parameters plus the independence requirement; the ontology is the parameters; the feedback is parameters-not-met routing to a re-dispatch. The drafted spec, which aimed the conditions partly at the prose, is re-pointed to the process.

## Delivery: an agent-able skill

TUNED ships as a writing-only skill that takes arguments in a fixed format (the target, concern, and intent as declared, ORE-graded inputs). Each run is a fresh, isolated agent carrying only the wrapper and the piece, so drift does not accumulate across a session. Feedback comes back as the author's edits, and a tuned agent is re-dispatched on the parts in question rather than the whole. Briefs stay calm and minimal, because over-briefing is itself pressure.

## Validation: the concrete run

Tested on a real Perplexity-plus-author article (a Paragraph piece). The wrapper de-slopped cleanly, then over-corrected into sterile (v1), then over-corrected into overblown-punchy (v2), and the author's placing landed it at v3: alive and grounded, punch sparing and earned. That full arc is the design working as designed: the tool reduces, its own bias shows in both directions, the mechanical bands bound it, and the human closes the gap. It also produced real calibration on the author's register that talking could not. And the author's catch of an over-claim (the first technology versus the first computer technology) showed the saying-not-thinking boundary live: the wrapper preserved the source's claim by design, and the author's disconfirmation discipline caught the over-reach.
